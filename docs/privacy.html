Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This application is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to Facebook Pages that the owner manages. It is not offered to the public.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal data of any Facebook user. It only uses the Page access tokens of the owner's own Pages to publish Reels and read their publishing status.

Data deletion

No user data is stored. To request deletion or ask a question, contact the Page owner through the Facebook Page's Messenger.
